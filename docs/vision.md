# Logistics Decision Intelligence Platform

## Vision

A SaaS Decision Intelligence Platform for logistics operations.

The platform complements existing Transportation Management Systems (TMS) by ingesting operational and telemetry data, detecting operational risks and opportunities, generating AI-assisted recommendations, and measuring their business impact.

Instead of replacing the customer's TMS, the platform acts as an intelligent decision layer that enriches operational data with analytics, optimization, and AI.

## Problem

Most logistics companies already operate mature TMS, ERP, Fleet Management, and Telematics solutions.

These systems excel at operational execution but provide limited support for operational decision-making.

Typical gaps include:

- intelligent dispatch recommendations
- proactive exception detection
- operational analytics
- AI-assisted explanations
- modern geospatial visualization

## Solution

The platform continuously synchronizes operational data from external systems.

It enriches that data with:

- telemetry
- analytics
- optimization
- AI reasoning

and produces actionable recommendations for dispatchers and operations managers.

Recommendation example:

Truck 17 - Driver John - Departure 10:40 - ETA 14:15 - Confidence 93%

Dispatcher reviews the recommendation and decides whether to apply it.

## Deployment Model

The platform primarily operates alongside an existing TMS.

Operational data is synchronized from customer systems through APIs, webhooks, file imports, and telematics integrations.

The platform maintains its own operational intelligence model optimized for analytics and decision-making.

Customers can consume insights through:

- Web dashboard
- REST APIs
- Webhooks
- MCP Server

The platform is not intended to replace:

- Transportation Management Systems
- Warehouse Management Systems

Instead, it provides a Decision Intelligence layer that transforms operational data into actionable decisions.
